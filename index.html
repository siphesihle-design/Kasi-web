Everything you need to know before you start.

K@si Web is a marketplace that helps you discover local barbers and salons, explore services and prices, and request appointments.
Yes. You can explore salon profiles and services before signing in. An account is needed when you want to submit a booking request.
Browse the salons, select a business and service, sign in, choose your preferred date and time, and submit your booking request. The salon can then respond to your request. A request is not a confirmed appointment until the business responds.
Check the salon's profile for pricing and payment details. Payment arrangements are made according to the business's instructions.
Barbers and salon owners can register using the business option on the account page to get started with K@si Web.
Service details, prices and opening times are provided by the businesses. Check the salon's current listing or contact the business directly before travelling.
